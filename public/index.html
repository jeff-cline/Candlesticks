The number that matters

A $25,000 funded account with a $1,500 trailing drawdown, traded at 4 full-size NQ contracts, is 18.75 points wide. That is the entire account, measured in the instrument you're trading. Nasdaq futures cover that in under a minute on an ordinary day.

The same four contracts in micros give you 187.5 points of room for identical entries and exits. The platform computes this for every account before an order can be staged, and shows it to you in points — not percentages you have to translate under pressure.

Compiled rule checks

  • Stop-loss and take-profit required on every order
  • Stop must not exceed 5× the profit target
  • Risk blocked when it exceeds an account's remaining room
  • Daily loss limit per account
  • Entries locked before the close and after the open
  • Breached accounts excluded from every allocation
